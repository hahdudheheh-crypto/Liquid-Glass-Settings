## 🌟 Overview

**Liquid Glass Settings** is a cutting-edge Xposed/LSPosed module designed exclusively for Samsung One UI. It completely revamps Samsung Settings into a breathtaking **3D Liquid Acrylic Glass** experience inspired by futuristic translucent design aesthetics.

Unlike ordinary "flat transparent" overlays, Liquid Glass delivers genuine optical depth, curved specular surface glares, luminous dual-layer caustic borders, and seamless card fusion across both the main Settings homepage and all SubSettings sub-pages.

## ✨ Features

- 💎 **Authentic 3D Liquid Glass Material:**
  - **Refractive Optical Core:** Smoky sapphire/cobalt gradient body that gives tangible physical density and presence rather than faint, washed-out transparency.
  - **Curved Specular Glare Arc:** 3D convex light reflection along the top curvature of cards replicating physical molded glass.
  - **Dual-Layer Caustic Rim:** Outer luminous electric-blue rim + inner 0.9dp refractive glass bevel for pronounced depth.
  - **Hairline Glass Dividers:** Delicate `0.8dp` translucent glass shelves between seamlessly fused preference rows.

- 📐 **100% Seamless Group Fusing (Zero Card Fragmentation):**
  - Canonical grouping for One UI 9 Settings Homepage (Samsung Account, Connections, Galaxy AI, Display, Battery, Accounts & Backup, Digital Wellbeing, Device Care, Apps, etc.).
  - Fuses multi-item categories seamlessly into singular rounded liquid cards with zero gaps or detached lines.
  - SubSettings category grouping dynamically mirrors One UI structure while keeping standalone settings as elegant rounded capsules.

- 🖼 **True Full-Screen Edge-to-Edge:**
  - Completely suppresses Samsung OEM horizontal padding (`mDrawHorizontalPadding` 30px black side gutters).
  - Eliminates black headers, `BackgroundFallback` dark bars, and framework corner mask overlays.

- 🔤 **Universal High-Contrast Typography:**
  - Hardware-accelerated drop shadow layer on all titles, subtitles, summaries, and captions.
  - 100% crystal-clear readability over **ANY** wallpaper (from deep dark space to bright white clouds and snow).

- 🎨 **Custom Wallpaper & Presets:**
  - Choose any custom image directly from your gallery with high-resolution center-crop scaling.
  - Built-in Samsung Cosmic Blue backdrop with glowing radial ambient spheres.
  - Interactive manager app with customizable opacity, blur radius, border intensity, and capsule corner radius.

- 🔍 **Liquid Floating Search Capsule & Relative Links:**
  - Floating bottom search bar styled as a glossy liquid glass pill.
  - Sub-page "Looking for something else?" transformed from an opaque dark box into an aesthetic floating liquid card.

## 📱 Compatibility

- **Samsung Devices:** Galaxy A, S, Z series running One UI 6.0, 7.0, 8.x, or 9.0 (Android 14 – 17).
- **Framework:** LSPosed Modern API 101/102 (e.g., Vector Active 2.2+ or official LSPosed).
- **Root:** Magisk, KernelSU, or APatch (required for LSPosed).

## 🚀 Installation & Usage

1. **Install Prerequisites:**
   - Ensure your device is rooted with Magisk / KernelSU / APatch.
   - Install and activate **LSPosed (Zygisk)**.

2. **Install Module:**
   - Download the latest `LiquidGlassSettings.apk` from the [Releases](https://github.com/haithemxt/Liquid-Glass-Settings/releases) page.
   - Install the APK on your Samsung device.

3. **Activate in LSPosed:**
   - Open **LSPosed Manager**.
   - Navigate to Modules and enable **Liquid Glass Settings**.
   - Make sure **Settings** (`com.android.settings`) is checked in the scope.

4. **Customize & Enjoy:**
   - Open the **Liquid Glass Settings** companion app from your launcher.
   - Adjust glass opacity, blur, corner radius, or pick a custom wallpaper from your gallery.
   - Tap **"Restart Settings (Root)"** to apply changes instantly.
